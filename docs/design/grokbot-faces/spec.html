Grok Bot on Stack-chan: face and motion spec

Motion is taken from the Grok Bot app's own mark engine (sprites.html, sampled at 20 Hz); body shape, eye shape and color are taken from the grey pill reference. Engine offsets are converted at 4.56 px per engine unit, and eye relative offsets are scaled by 0.84 (the pill's eye height over the engine's) and clamped so the eyes never leave the pill. Eyes are holes in the body color, exactly as the engine draws them. Every number in the face and timing columns is measured; triggers, servo poses and exits are this spec's inference, because Grok Bot never streams its state to a device.

eye placement

blink
close 100 ms to 25 % height, hold 75 ms, open 100 ms; every 2.0 to 2.9 s
swell
body scale 1.00 to 1.067 and back over 2.5 s, once per idle cycle; continuous 5.6 s when sleeping
speech
no mouth; while TTS plays, squash along the pill's short axis: sx = 1 + 0.04a, sy = 1 − 0.14a, a = audio level at 20 Hz
servos
yaw ±40°, pitch −10° to +25°, 30°/s quadratic ease in out; hold during TTS

Where the pill changes the engine

Only the body takes the pill shape. When the engine shrinks the body (Thinking, Waiting) the shrunken body stays a pill, while dots and particles stay round. Orbit planes inherit the −15° tilt. Because the eye pair sits up and to the right on the pill, glance and gaze offsets are clamped: upward travel is cut to about 15 px and rightward travel to about 25 px, so motion that went off the circle in the engine stays inside the capsule here.

Color

Default is the reference grey #777777 on #121212. The ink tweak on each board swaps the body color to mirror a Bot's chosen color; eyes stay the ground color so blink and gaze read the same on every ink.
